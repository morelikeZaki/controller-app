Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Controller is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage their own YouTube channels.

Data it accesses

With the owner's permission, Controller uses Google's read-only scopes (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly) to read the owner's channel details, video list and video statistics.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to show the owner their own upload schedule and statistics. It is stored on the owner's own computer and their own phone. It is not sold, shared, transferred to anyone else, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

Controller's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
