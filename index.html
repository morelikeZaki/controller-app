Controller

Controller is a private, single-user tool that helps one creator plan and track uploads to their own YouTube channels. It runs on the creator's own computer and phone.

What it does with YouTube

  • Reads the creator's own channel and video statistics (read-only access).
  • Detects when a planned video has been posted.
  • It never uploads, edits, deletes or posts anything on YouTube.
